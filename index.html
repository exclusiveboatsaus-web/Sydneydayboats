How it works

  1. Tell us about your day. Group size, preferred date and the kind of day you want.
  2. Get a clear quote. Transparent pricing so you know what you are paying.
  3. Lock in your date. We confirm the boat, skipper and meeting point.
  4. Show up and enjoy. We handle the skipper, safety and logistics. You handle the fun.

What a typical day looks like

Board at a harbour wharf, cruise the iconic sights, anchor in a sheltered bay for a swim, then food, cake and celebration time before cruising home as the light changes. Start times and itineraries are flexible.

Why private beats shared

A private charter means your playlist, your guest list and your pace, with space to celebrate without strangers. It is the difference between attending an event and owning the day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a private boat charter cost?

Pricing depends on group size, duration and season. Send us your details and we will give you a clear quote.

Can we swim?

Yes. On suitable daytime charters we anchor in calm spots around the harbour, weather and conditions permitting.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Ask us when you enquire and we will confirm what is included and what you can bring.

How long are the charters?

Four hours is a popular choice for cruising, swimming and celebrating. Longer or shorter charters can be arranged.

Where do we board?

We arrange a convenient harbour pick-up point. Tell us where your group is coming from when you enquire.

What if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. Your skipper will adjust the plan for comfort, and we will talk through your options if conditions are unsuitable.

Ready to plan your day on the harbour?

Tell us a few details and we will reply with options and a clear quote. No obligation.
